The idea

One ecosystem, fewer chargers

The pitch behind this concept storefront: a phone, tablet, watch and buds that all talk to each other, so notes taken on the Tab show up on the phone, and a workout logged on the Watch syncs without opening three different apps.

  • Lecture halls — S Pen note-taking on the Tab S10, synced instantly.
  • Between classes — ANC buds for the library, watch for step tracking.
  • Dorm nights — Neo QLED TV with a built-in gaming hub.
